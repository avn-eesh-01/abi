General & Eligibility

The Affiliation Bureau serves as an administrative and regulatory body responsible for evaluating, inspecting, and granting official affiliation status to educational institutions. We ensure that schools meet established standards in infrastructure, academic quality, governance, safety, and regulatory compliance.

Affiliation applications are open to educational institutions managed by: Registered Educational Trusts or Registered Societies or Section 8 Non-Profit Companies, Government and Quasi-Government Bodies.
Note: Private individuals operating for personal profit without a registered non-profit trust/society/entity are not eligible.

We process affiliations across all primary and secondary educational levels: Pre-Primary / Primary Level (Nursery to Class V) to Senior Secondary Level (Class XI & XII - Science, Commerce, and Humanities Streams) in School Education and other Institutions, such as: Management Colleges, Law Colleges, Engineering Colleges, Para – Medical/Medical Colleges etc.

Application Process & Tracking

All applications must be submitted digitally through our official portal:

  1. Register: Create an institutional account using your Trust/Society details.
  2. Fill Form: Complete the online affiliation application form.
  3. Upload Documents: Attach required safety NOCs, land deeds, and structural certificates.
  4. Pay Fee: Submit the non-refundable processing fee via the payment gateway.
  5. Submit: Lock and submit the application for desk audit.

The complete processing timeline typically spans 45 to 60 business days from the date of final submission, subject to prompt document verification and the completion of physical/virtual site inspections.

Yes. Upon submitting your application, an Application Reference Number (ARN) will be generated. You can log into your Portal Dashboard at any time to monitor real-time updates across all evaluation stages: Desk Audit, Document Verification, Inspection Scheduled, Board Review and Approval/Issuance.

Compliance, Documents & Infrastructure

Before starting your application, ensure you have valid, scanned copies of: Trust/Society/Section 8 Company Registration Certificate & Bylaws, State Government No-Objection Certificate (NOC) / Recognition Certificate, Registered Land Title Deed along with Long-term Lease Agreement, Land Safety & Land Use Certificate from Competent Revenue Authority, Building Safety Certificate issued by PWD / Registered Structural Engineer, Fire Safety Certificate from the State Fire Services Department, Health & Sanitation Certificate from Local Municipal Authority.

While exact norms depend on the Bye Laws of the Board/Council according to the location (Metropolitan vs. Non-Metropolitan areas), baseline requirements include:

  • Land Area: Minimum contiguous land parcel (typically 2.0 acres, or as specified under local/urban norms).
  • Boundary Wall: Pucca boundary wall surrounding the entire school/institution's campus.
  • Facilities: Standard-sized classrooms, Science/Maths laboratories, computer labs, library, separate hygienic toilets for boys and girls, drinking water facilities, and play fields.

Initial Affiliation: Granted for a period of 3 to 5 academic years.
Extension / Renewal: Schools must apply for extension of affiliation at least 6 months prior to the expiry date by submitting an updated compliance report and undergoing periodic reassessment.
