OUR PROCESS

A Simple & Transparent Process

We make affiliation simple with a step-by-step approach.

Consultation

We begin with a detailed consultation to understand your institution, objectives, eligibility requirements, and the most suitable affiliation pathway.

01
02

Documentation

Our team organizes, drafts, and reviews all required documents, annexures, undertakings, and compliance records for a complete submission set.

Application

Once the paperwork is finalized, we prepare the official application package and support accurate filing through the designated authority or portal.

03
04

Inspection

We help you prepare for inspection by verifying infrastructure, records, staffing, and presentation readiness ahead of the regulatory visit.

Approval

After review and compliance validation, we assist with follow-ups, clarifications, and deficiency responses until approval is processed.

06

Affiliation

The process concludes with formal affiliation or recognition being granted, along with guidance on the next compliance and operational steps.
