What We Help You Build, Approve, and Manage

Each engagement is structured around regulatory readiness, documentation quality, and a clear approval pathway.

School Affiliation

Guidance for CBSE, ICSE, State Board, NIOS, and other school affiliation pathways.

  • Eligibility review and affiliation roadmap
  • Infrastructure and staffing readiness checks
  • Documentation and application support

College Affiliation

Support for academic, technical, and professional colleges seeking approvals and university linkage.

  • Affiliation planning with universities and councils
  • Norm-based compliance and course readiness
  • Submission tracking and clarification support

University Consultancy

Advisory for private, deemed, and state university setup under UGC-aligned requirements.

  • Establishment strategy and legal structuring
  • Project reports, statutes, and governance documents
  • Regulatory sequencing from concept to approval

AICTE Assistance

Technical institution support for approval applications, infrastructure norms, and compliance reviews.

  • Approval calendar and intake planning
  • Land, lab, and faculty norm verification
  • Portal filing and response management

UGC Consultancy

Specialized guidance for Section 2(f), 12(B), and broader UGC recognition requirements.

  • Recognition strategy and eligibility mapping
  • Academic and administrative compliance checks
  • Representation support through the process

Professional Council Approvals

End-to-end assistance for pharmacy, nursing, medical, and other council-governed institutions.

  • PCI, INC, NMC, and sector-specific readiness
  • Facility, hospital, and curriculum requirement review
  • Inspection and deficiency response preparation

Documentation Support

Inspection Preparation

Mock inspections, pre-visit audits, and institution readiness before official evaluation.

  • Team briefing before inspection day

Recognition Assistance
